\begin{abstract}
  \pan We are the most modern parody of a long-established dictionary.  We relate the language of characters with the language of finite-dimensional complex group modules. The main meat of this document is in subsection \ref{sec:translations-themselves} where we draw direct parallels between properties of groups or their representations to properties of the characters, and in subsection \ref{sec:grammar} where we collect some facts of the characters themselves. A fiction of the story of characters is provided (mostly for fun, though I hope it builds an appreciation for why characters should be useful and natural). All proofs and some character tables are included.
\end{abstract}
